About

Why Sonora exists.

Making music shouldn't depend on what you can afford. Sonora is a studio anyone can download, use, read and improve.

In the author's words

Don't get me wrong: Sonora isn't a rip-off of Ableton Live. It's a completely new program, started from scratch, free and open source, so that everyone can produce music.

It looks like Live on purpose. I want people to get up to speed without having to learn a new program. And although it can open Live projects, that's not what I recommend it for. Start new music with it.

It moves fast, and it's built around what producers have been asking for forever: built-in collaboration over the internet, built-in AI with an architecture made for agents, and instruments that would otherwise cost a lot. Delta updates keep the improvements coming.

If you're a professional, or you have the money, go ahead and buy Ableton Live. It's amazing software, and you'll get support. Otherwise, try Sonora, and help me make it better.

Wellington Chaves, who started Sonora
The author

An architect who loves music.

Sonora was started by Wellington Chaves. He has spent about 20 years as an enterprise architect at IBM and Kyndryl. Outside work, he's a music enthusiast who makes music as a hobby.

Sonora brings the two together: an architect's way of building systems, applied to a music studio that anyone can use.

Built in the open

Sonora is free software, built in public on GitHub, with the help of musicians who try it and tell us what's wrong. The plan is to grow it as a community of music enthusiasts.

How it's built

Original code, all the way down.

From scratch in C++

Sonora's audio engine, devices, instruments and interface are its own code, built with the open-source JUCE framework for audio and Skia for drawing.

Interoperable, not copied

Sonora opens Live sets by reading the documented file format. It contains no Ableton code, presets, samples or fonts, and every screenshot on this site is Sonora's own interface.

Open source

Everything is published under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later. Anyone can read it, build it, and keep it alive.

Your music stays yours

No account, no telemetry. Your projects stay on your Mac unless you choose to share a session.

Support Sonora

Free for you. Not free to make.

Sonora is free and will stay free: no price, no account, no subscription. Keeping it going does cost something every month.

  • Hosting. The website, the downloads and the servers that build every release.
  • Collaboration servers. The relay and cloud that connect producers working on the same project over the internet.
  • AI tools. Sonora moves this fast because it's built with the help of AI, and those tools aren't free. Without them, this project wouldn't be possible.
  • My time. Evenings and weekends, given to this project with a lot of love. :)

If Sonora helps you make music, a coffee helps keep it going. It's never required: everything stays free either way. Monthly supporters on Ko-fi can have their name in Sonora's About window and on this page.
